## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision? Include the relevant requirements,
constraints, and forces in tension. Write for a reader who has not been part of
the discussion. State facts and trade-offs, not the conclusion.

## Decision

What are we doing? Use the active voice: "We will …". Be specific enough that
someone can act on it. If the decision has parts, enumerate them.

## Consequences

What becomes easier as a result? What becomes harder? What are we now committed
to, and what will we need to keep an eye on? Include negative consequences
honestly — every real decision has them.

## Alternatives considered

The other options that were genuinely on the table, each with a sentence or two
on why it wasn't chosen. If an alternative is a reasonable future reconsideration,
say so and under what conditions.
